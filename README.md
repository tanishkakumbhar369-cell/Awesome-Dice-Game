## About
Awesome Dice Game is a simple and addictive dice game where players take turns rolling dice to earn points. Depending on the game variant, you may play solo against the computer or with friends locally. The rules are designed to be easy to learn while still offering room for strategy.

## Objective
Be the first player to reach the target score (commonly 50 or 100 points), or finish the most rounds with the highest score — depending on the game mode you choose.

## How to Play
Basic flow (may vary by game variant):
1. Players take turns.
2. On your turn, press "Roll" to roll the dice.
3. The dice value(s) are added to your turn total.
4. You can choose to "Hold" (bank your turn total into your overall score) or "Roll" again to try to increase your turn total.
5. Some variants have a penalty for rolling certain values (for example, rolling a 1 may forfeit your turn total).
6. Play proceeds to the next player after you end your turn or hit a penalty.

If the game includes special rules or power-ups, see the in-game help or settings screen for details.

## Controls
- Roll: Roll the dice for the current turn.
- Hold / Bank: Save your current turn total to your overall score and end your turn.
- New Game: Start a fresh game (resets all scores).
- Settings: Change target score, number of dice, AI difficulty, or other options (if available).

(Buttons may be labeled differently depending on the platform/version.)

## Scoring
- Each roll adds the face value of the die/dice to your turn total.
- When you Hold, your turn total is added to your overall score.
- Penalties (if present): Rolling a specific face (e.g., 1) may cause you to lose your turn total or trigger other effects — see the variant rules.

## Winning the Game
- The game ends when a player reaches or exceeds the target score (e.g., 100), or when a predefined number of rounds complete.
- The player with the highest score wins.

## Tips & Strategy
- Be mindful of risk vs reward: pushing for a bigger turn total may backfire if penalties are in play.
- Adjust your playstyle when you’re behind: take smarter risks to catch up.
- Pay attention to opponent patterns — if they play conservatively, sometimes bold plays can give you the advantage.
